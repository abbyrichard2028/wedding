The Venue

The CAPE

An 1863 Georgian manor on Picton’s Mansion Row, restored as a boutique hotel and event space, with sweeping lawns, mature trees and a wraparound verandah. It sits one block from Picton Harbour, within walking distance of Main Street’s restaurants and shops.

Travel & Stay

Make a weekend of it in the County

By car

Picton is roughly 2½–3 hours east of Toronto. Most guests drive via Highway 401, exiting at Belleville and heading south into Prince Edward County. Summer weekend traffic can add time, so plan to arrive early.

By train or air

The nearest VIA Rail station is Belleville, about 35 minutes’ drive away. The closest airport is Kingston (YGK), a little over an hour’s drive; Toronto Pearson is the main option for most flights. [Shuttle details, if any.]
